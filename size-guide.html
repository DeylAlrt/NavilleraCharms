Size Guide

How to Measure Your Wrist (cm)

  1. Wrap a measuring tape or string around your wrist where you wear your bracelet.
  2. It should feel snug, not tight.
  3. Measure in centimeters (cm) and check the guide below.
Size Links Wrist Size (cm) Description
Small 16 14–15 cm Best for petite wrists or a snug fit
Medium 18 16–17 cm Most popular size. Comfortable for everyday wear
Large 20 18–19 cm For wider wrists or a relaxed fit
XL 22 20–21 cm For wide wrists only. Not recommended for medium sizes or extra space

Size Tips

Not sure which size to choose?
We recommend 18 links as a standard fit.

For a snug fit, choose 1 link less. For a breathable fit, choose the exact size listed above.
